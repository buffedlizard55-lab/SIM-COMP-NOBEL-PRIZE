## Data-quality flags

- **close_time_excluded** (warning): Settled Nobel markets carry a close_time written after the announcement (e.g. KXNOBELECON-25 closes 2025-10-17 with sub-second precision). No strategy reads close_time; the schedule channel uses only whole-minute event strike_date values, which Nobel events do not publish.
- **panel_selection_by_lifetime_volume** (warning): The settled panel keeps the top 25 markets per series by lifetime volume, chosen at fetch time. That is post-selected: a strategy cannot have known at decision time which markets would be kept.
- **cash_not_recycled** (info): Settlement is applied after the whole decision loop, so settled cash never funds later trades inside a run. Sizing rules can only size down; the $300 per-market cap and 500-contract clip bind.
- **nobel_subjects_without_settled_history** (warning): KXNOBELCHEM, KXNOBELMED, KXNOBELPHYSICS trade in the forward universe but have no settled event in the snapshot, so no settled-universe result says anything about those subjects.
- **event_field_incomplete** (warning): KXNOBELECON-25: 20 stored contracts all settled NO. The winning contract (if any) is not in the snapshot, so event-structure rules see a partial field.
